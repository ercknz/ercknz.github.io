About

I'm a biomedical engineer with a mechanical engineering background. I build robotic and wearable systems for people recovering from stroke and other mobility impairments, from the CAD model and machined parts to the control code and the human-subject study.

I earned my PhD in Biomedical Engineering at NJIT in 2026 with the dissertation Towards a Data-Driven and Mechatronics Framework for Post-Stroke Motor Rehabilitation. Since 2022 I've been Director of Laboratories for NJIT's Biomedical Engineering Department. I run the 3D printing studio, machine shop, and teaching labs, and help student teams turn capstone designs into working prototypes.

Projects

Dissertation · Exoskeletons

2-DOF Powered Ankle-Foot System

A powered foot and ankle that lets a lower-extremity exoskeleton balance and walk more like a person. It drives dorsiflexion/plantarflexion and inversion/eversion through two posterior custom linear actuators working differentially, and an integrated force plate tracks center of pressure.

  • 2 DOF
  • Custom linear actuators
  • Center-of-pressure sensing
Watch the demo video WeRob 2017 paper
Dissertation · Upper-limb rehabilitation

3-DOF Robotic Arm Support

A seat-mounted robotic arm support that attaches at the wrist and runs an admittance control loop, so the robot follows the force the user applies. It's backdrivable, so the user can still move it safely if power is lost. Validated in experiments with human subjects.

  • 3 DOF
  • Admittance control
  • Dynamixel XM540
  • Teensy 4.1
  • ATI Mini45 F/T sensor
Control code on GitHub
Dissertation · Clinical machine learning

Smartwatch Prognostic Modeling After Stroke

Machine-learning models that predict arm usage after stroke based on clinical scores, from smartwatches. Built in MATLAB and trained on NJIT's Wulver high-performance computing cluster.

  • MATLAB
  • Wearable accelerometry
  • HPC
Wearable sensing

Gait Speed Estimation

Algorithms that estimate walking speed from accelerometers on the ankles and hips, giving clinicians a quick, low-cost measure. The final method isolates heel-strike, mid-stance, toe-off, and mid-swing to build an average step profile. Tested in people with varying levels of mobility impairment.

  • MATLAB
  • IMUs
  • Gait events
MATLAB code on GitHub EMBC 2020 paper
Collaboration · Exoskeleton control

Learning-Based Exoskeleton Control

With collaborators at NJIT and Kessler Foundation, I contributed to deep reinforcement learning controllers for a lower-limb rehabilitation exoskeleton, covering squat assistance and robust walking with a musculoskeletal model in the loop.

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Musculoskeletal modeling
JNER 2023 Frontiers 2021
Teaching labs

EMG Robot Car & MATLAB Lab Tools

Hands-on labs for junior-year biomedical engineering students. Students drive a small robot car with their own muscle signals, recorded on Biopac hardware and sent wirelessly to an ESP32. Companion MATLAB interfaces handle force-tracing exercises and synchronize Biopac recordings with motion capture.

  • Biopac MP36
  • ESP32-S3
  • ESP-NOW
  • MATLAB GUIs
